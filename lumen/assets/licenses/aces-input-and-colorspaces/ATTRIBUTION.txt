CutCat modifications in lumen/apple_log.py: translated the transfer equations
into vectorized NumPy with explicit validation and overflow bounds. Replaced
the ACES/AP0 conversion route with a direct D65 BT.2020-to-BT.709 matrix derived
from primary chromaticities. Added an original fixed SDR luminance shoulder,
neutral-axis gamut compression and BT.709 output encoding. This is not an ACES
output transform or an Apple LUT. No Apple LUT is included or redistributed.
